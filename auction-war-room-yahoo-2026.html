Know the room

Yahoo is a different auction than ESPN

The settings, and what they do to prices

  • Half-PPR. A catch is worth half a point, so the volume premium shrinks: target hogs and pass-catching backs (St. Brown, Nacua, Achane, Kamara) lose a few dollars; touchdown and yardage backs (Henry, Kyren Williams, Taylor, Cook, Montgomery) gain them. Elite TEs lose a touch of separation.
  • 2 WR + FLEX, not 3 WR. Same starting shape as ESPN — nine starters — so WR depth is not a mandate. RB stays the scarcest asset.
  • 15-man roster (6 bench) instead of 16. One fewer $1 slot: max bid is $186, average per slot is $13.33. Your bench darts matter more, not less — there are fewer of them.
  • Two IR slots. A genuinely different weapon: injured stashes (Tyson) don't cost you a bench spot. Draft for it.
  • Playoffs weeks 15–17 by default (confirm in your league settings). Week 14 byes still bite.

Auction 101, second edition

The rules — plus what your mock taught you

The seven rules, tightened

Write max prices before you draft — the "pay up to" column below is that list. Never go more than $2–3 over on a tier-1 target.
Nominate players you don't want. Cook, Taylor, Henry, and McCaffrey are the perfect openers in a Yahoo room: the sheet tells everyone they're worth $40+.
Never show your hand early. Chase and JSN are your targets? They surface after three or four RBs have drained the big spenders.
Three studs, not four. Cap the elite spend at ~$150–155. The fourth stud is a luxury that costs you WR2 and TE.
Watch the room's money. Yahoo shows every team's remaining budget. When three teams are under $20 with ten spots to fill, everything left is on sale.
Keep $30–40 for the rounds 5–7 window — planned, not leftover. That's where Lamar, LaPorta, McConkey, Loveland, and the WR2s sell for half their value.
Keep $8–12 for the endgame and spend all $200. A $0 max offer means every $2–4 player is unwinnable by rule.

  • Shape: the problem. $185 on four players left $15 for twelve spots and a $1 max bid from round 5 on. In that room Lamar went for $10, LaPorta $5, McConkey $15, Jameson Williams $17 — your two holes (WR2, TE) sold for lunch money while you couldn't bid.
  • Dodged: Team 2's $53 St. Brown and $41 Saquon; Team 5 leaving $47 unspent; the $24 Allen war.
  • The fix: drop the fourth stud. Same room, minus Chase Brown: Jameson Williams $18 + LaPorta $6 + $9 endgame ammo is a strictly stronger lineup.
  • Yahoo twist: the room will overpay RBs by sheet, so your three studs should probably be two elite WRs and one RB — the reverse of your mock.

What to nominate, in order — Yahoo edition

Openers (your first 3–4 turns): the RBs Yahoo's sheet overprices — Taylor, Cook, Henry, McCaffrey. The room sees $41–50 on screen and bids to it. Not your money.
Early-middle: hype names just below elite — Jeanty (the ankle will spark debate), Saquon, Jeremiyah Love. Keep draining budgets.
Middle: float your real targets one at a time — Chase, JSN, Puka if they haven't come up, then Lamar, McConkey, Loveland, Lloyd — while the RB spenders sit at $15.
Late: nominate kickers and defenses while other managers still have money; every $2 they spend there is $2 that can't outbid your Godwin/Sutton/Stribling endgame.
Warning, still: price enforcement is how you end up owning a $38 James Cook. Bid only what you'd happily pay.

Logistics

Yahoo room notes & the last 72 hours

Know your Yahoo room

  • Defaults: $200 cap, $1 minimum, 15 spots all bought at auction, half-PPR, 4-point passing TDs. Confirm scoring and roster in League Settings before the draft — a commissioner tweak to 3 WR or full PPR changes this board.
  • The player list defaults to XRank (Yahoo's analysts + RotoWire blend) with a static dollar value beside it. Most of your room drafts off that column. The Yahoo-mispricing table above is your map of where it's wrong.
  • Yahoo enforces your max bid and shows every team's remaining budget in the draft room. Live in that panel.
  • Short bid clock, resets on every bid. Ceilings get decided before nomination, never during the countdown.
  • Autodraft is unforgiving: if you drop, Yahoo bids conservatively off its own values. Pre-rank your list (Draft → Pre-Draft Rankings) and keep the app open as a backup.
  • Two IR slots mean a $1 Tyson stash costs no bench space. Nabers is not IR-eligible unless designated — that risk needs a real bench body behind it.

Between now and the draft

  • Thursday/Friday injury reports are the last word. Re-check Nabers, Egbuka, Jeanty, Pickens, and Puka on the final report before the draft; a downgrade moves each $5–10.
  • Mark 20–25 targets across price bands with your personal max — the "pay up to" column is the starting draft.
  • Write two anchor paths: (A) Chase + JSN + Chase Brown ≈ $135; (B) Gibbs + Chase ≈ $118 + a $30 RB2. Both leave $35 for the lull.
  • Budget $8–12 for the endgame — Godwin, Sutton, Boutte, Stribling, Okonkwo are all sub-$6 players who start games in October.
  • $1 kicker, $1 defense. Nominate everyone else's early.
  • Week 1 is Thursday. If the auction is late in the week, players on Thursday-night teams need to be drafted before kickoff — check your draft time against the schedule.
